How the times are computed
  • Opkomsttijd = dispatch + turn-out (uitruktijd) + drive time, as in the VRU dekkingsplan.
  • Turn-out times from the VRU dekkingsplan 2023-2024: Amersfoort-Centrum TS1 1:38, Amersfoort-Noord professional 1:26, volunteer 5:11.
  • The VRU does not publish its dispatch time or road speeds (TomTom speed profiles). The default speeds and the 60 s dispatch were tuned so this page reproduces the VRU's own results: 96.2% of Amersfoort within 10 minutes with Olympus oost, 90.7% with west.
  • Olympus oost and west are estimates: oost by the roundabout on the Bergenboulevard (the VRU mentions "de rotonde"), west by the Lindeboomseweg. Drag them if you know the exact plots.
  • The drive goes to the nearest road point of each address. Roads without addresses are coloured too.
  • The VRU scores the median time per CBS buurt. This page counts individual addresses, so it shows where the norm is met, not the official buurt verdict.
